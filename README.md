# 📚 PhantomNotes

**Transform your documents into intelligent summaries**

![AI-Powered](https://img.shields.io/badge/AI-Powered-blue?style=for-the-badge)
![Privacy First](https://img.shields.io/badge/Privacy-First-green?style=for-the-badge)
![Free to Use](https://img.shields.io/badge/100%25-Free-brightgreen?style=for-the-badge)

PhantomNotes is an AI-powered document summarization tool that transforms PDFs and text into intelligent, context-aware summaries. Whether you're a student, researcher, business professional, or casual reader, PhantomNotes tailors summaries to your specific needs.

🌐 **Live Demo**: [phantom-notes.onrender.com](https://phantom-notes.onrender.com/)

---

## ✨ Features

### 🤖 AI-Powered Analysis
Advanced natural language processing to understand and summarize your documents with context and precision.

### 📄 Multiple Input Methods
- **PDF Upload** - Upload documents up to 50MB
- **Text Paste** - Directly paste any text for instant summarization

### 🎯 10+ Specialized Summary Types

1. **Student - Study Notes** 📖
   - Key concepts and definitions
   - Study-friendly formatting
   - Important points highlighted

2. **Novel Reader - Plot & Characters** 📚
   - Character analysis
   - Plot summaries
   - Theme identification

3. **Researcher - Methodology & Findings** 🔬
   - Research methodology breakdown
   - Key findings and conclusions
   - Statistical insights

4. **Business - Action Items & Decisions** 💼
   - Actionable takeaways
   - Key decisions made
   - Strategic insights

5. **Legal - Clauses & Terms** ⚖️
   - Important clauses highlighted
   - Legal terminology explained
   - Contract key points

6. **Technical - Architecture & APIs** 💻
   - System architecture overview
   - API documentation summaries
   - Technical specifications

7. **News - 5W1H Summary** 📰
   - Who, What, When, Where, Why, How
   - Concise news breakdown
   - Quick fact extraction

8. **Meeting Notes - Decisions & Actions** 🗓️
   - Meeting decisions
   - Action items with owners
   - Follow-up tasks

9. **Quick Overview** ⚡
   - Ultra-fast summaries
   - Main points only
   - Time-saving format

10. **Detailed Analysis** 🔍
    - Comprehensive breakdown
    - In-depth insights
    - Full context preservation

### 📤 Export Options
- **Multiple formats** - Export summaries in various formats
- **Easy sharing** - Share or save your summaries
- **Print-friendly** - Clean formatting for printing

### 🔒 Privacy & Security
- **Automatic deletion** - Files deleted after 1 hour
- **No permanent storage** - Your documents aren't stored
- **Secure processing** - HTTPS encrypted connections

## 🐛 Known Limitations

- PDF files with images only (scanned documents) may have reduced accuracy
- Complex mathematical equations may not parse perfectly
- Non-English text support is limited
- Large documents (>50MB) cannot be processed
